Steps:
0. trimesh -> quad mesh
1. initial global smoothing
2. iterative update until user criterion:
	a. for n iterations:
		i.  local optimization until none available
		ii. local coarsening operation
	b. local smoothing
3. global smoothing


Local operations:
- optimizing operations:
	- edge rotate
	- vertex rotate
- coarsening operations:
	- diagonal collapse
	- edge collapse
- cleaning operations:
	- doublet removal
	- singlet removal


Operation prioritization:
- collapses:
	edge collapses and diagonal collapses are handled together:
	collapse the shortest element of the mesh
	length of the diagonals are divided by sqrt(2) before any comparison
- optimizations:
	- vertex rotation:
	  calculate profitability: sum(edge lengths) - sum(diagonal lengths)
	  choose vertex with the highest profitability
	- edge rotation:
	  calculate profitability sum(|new e/d| - |old e/d|) for all new elements
	  the 3 new elements: the edge and two diagonals
	  choose rotation with the highest profitability
- cleanings:
	- doublet removal:
	  they are not removed explicitly (only with a diagonal collapse if needed)
	- singlet removal:
	  removed as soon as they appear (they are always degenerate, unlike doublets)


Tangent space smoothing
increase homeometry by moving vertices (they stay on the surface of the original mesh)
if e cannot be made homeometric -> good candidate for the next collapse/rotate
local: only affect a small area around the regions affected by the prev local operations
global: performed until convergence
schedule updated vertices for smoothing, if moved too much (>eps), schedule its neighbors too


Implementation details
- prioritizing operations:
	heap of potential operations sorted by prerecorded priorities
	perform a local operation -> update only the opers of neighbors (only they are affected)
